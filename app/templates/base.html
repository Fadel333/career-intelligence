The Problem We're Solving

A quiet crisis unfolding across Ghana and West Africa

For Students & Graduates

  • Graduate without knowing which skills employers actually want
  • Spend months applying for jobs with no structured feedback
  • No localized African platform provides real-time market intelligence
  • Outdated curricula that lag behind industry evolution by years

For Employers & Universities

  • Companies report graduates aren't job-ready despite holding degrees
  • Career centers lack data tools for evidence-based guidance
  • Skills gap is widening faster than education systems can adapt
  • Expensive recruitment processes with mismatched candidates

Our Solution

TalentForge AI is an AI-powered talent intelligence platform that reads the real labor market — pulling live data from LinkedIn, Jobberman, Indeed, Glassdoor, Brighter Monday, company career pages, and more — then helps students understand exactly what skills they need, what gaps exist, and how to close them fast.

This is not a career advice chatbot. It's an intelligent, evidence-based, data-driven employability engine — localized for Africa.

How It Works

From CV to Career Roadmap — in 4 intelligent steps

1

Upload Your CV

Upload your CV in PDF or DOCX format. Our AI extracts your skills, experience, certifications, and education instantly.

2

AI-Powered Skill Extraction

Our NLP engine identifies your skills, categorizes them, and builds a comprehensive skill profile with your employability score.

3

Market Gap Analysis

We compare your skills against real-time job market data from 10+ platforms to identify your unique skill gaps and growth opportunities.

4

Get Your Career Roadmap

Receive a personalized learning roadmap with recommended courses, certifications, projects, and job matches to accelerate your career.
